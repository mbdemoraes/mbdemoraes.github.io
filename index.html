I study how artificial intelligence can learn in low-data scenarios and be combined with evolutionary algorithms to solve complex and time-consuming nonlinear optimization problems.

I am a Professor at the School of Technology (FT) of the University of Campinas (Unicamp). Before joining FT/Unicamp as a faculty member in late 2026, I was a researcher at the Renato Archer Information Technology Center, a unit of Brazil's Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation.
